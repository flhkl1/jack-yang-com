About

I'm a senior at UC Berkeley studying Computer Science, Mathematics, and German, and research engineer at the UCSF NeuroAI Lab. I've previously worked on agents that manage capacity routing at AWS Bedrock and data pipelines at livex.ai, as well as fundamental investment research at Ocean Arete.

Previous work

The gold standard for staging dementia in the real world is a semistructured interview called the Clinical Dementia Rating (CDR). At UCSF, we built an agent to simulate the interviewer administering the CDR, and I helped build the benchmark and its supporting infrastructure to assess its performance. My role was to use clinical judgment and gold-standard real-life CDR interviews to select features that distinguish a "good" and "natural" interview from a poorly administered one, and then build durable, modular agentic harnesses that could keep pace with rapid model development.
