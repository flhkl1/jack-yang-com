Idea

I like taking care of myself in 4 simple ways:

1. Eating well

2. Sleeping consistently

3. Exercising

4. Keeping relationships with good people

all without overspending

Problem

A few observations:

My Garmin logs data but I don't extract insights from it.

When I'm strapped for time, I eat unhealthily.

When I travel, I often miss people I would have liked to hang out with. A lot of the times I find out they're in the same city after I leave!

I tend to overspend.

Solution

A dashboard that integrates other apps and unifies this information and sends push notifications.

Health pulls my sleep, steps and workouts from Garmin every day and sets a continuous meal plan against my macro targets.

CRM that updates where my friends are based on locations that they publicly share

Finance links my bank and brokerage accounts and analyzes where my money goes and sends push notifs when it believes I'm overspending.
